Wiz PDF Master — Privacy Policy

Wiz Labs · Effective 8 November 2026

100% offline · No data collected

Summary

Wiz PDF Master scans documents, creates PDFs, reads PDFs and recognises text entirely on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Camera

The camera is used only while you are scanning, to find page edges and take the photo you ask for. Images are processed on the device and saved inside the app's private storage.

Photos and files

When you import photos or open a PDF, you choose the file using Android's own picker. The app only gets access to the files you pick. PDFs you open from other apps are read in place and are not copied or uploaded.

Text recognition (OCR)

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit's on-device model, which is bundled inside the app. Recognition runs locally; no images or text are sent to Google or anyone else.

Passwords

If you unlock a password-protected PDF, the password is used only in memory to open that file and is never saved.

Storage and deletion

Your scans are stored in the app's private folder on your phone. Deleting a document in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Backups of app data are turned off.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save to device, the PDF goes only where you send it. What happens after that is governed by the app or service you choose.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact Wiz Labs through the developer contact on the Google Play listing.
