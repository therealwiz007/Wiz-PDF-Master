Wiz PDF Master turns your phone into a document scanner, a fast PDF reader and a text reader — all in one app, and everything works offline. Nothing you scan or open ever leaves your phone.

SCAN DOCUMENTS
• Live page-edge detection: point the camera and the page outline appears instantly
• Auto capture takes the photo for you when the page is steady
• Single or batch mode for multi-page documents, torch and tap-to-focus
• Adjust corners precisely with a built-in magnifier
• Perspective correction straightens tilted pages
• Five looks: Magic (removes shadows and whitens paper), Original, Grayscale, Black & White and No Shadow
• Rotate, reorder, delete and add pages before saving
• Import photos from your gallery and turn them into a PDF
• Save as A4, Letter or fit-to-image PDF

MAKE SCANS SEARCHABLE (OCR)
• Text recognition runs on your phone — no upload, no internet
• Saved scans get an invisible text layer, so you can search them and copy text
• Extract text from any page of any PDF and copy or share it

READ ANY PDF
• Open PDFs from your phone, WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive or any file manager
• Smooth scrolling, pinch-zoom and double-tap zoom with sharp re-rendering
• Search inside documents with highlighted results
• Page thumbnails, page slider and go-to-page
• Night reading mode for comfortable reading in the dark
• Opens where you left off
• Unlock password-protected PDFs (the password is never stored)

ORGANISE
• All your scans in one place with thumbnails
• Rename, share, save to device or delete
• Recent PDFs list for files opened from other apps
• Search your documents by name

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission — the app physically cannot send your documents anywhere
• No ads, no account, no tracking, no data collection
• Your scans are stored privately inside the app
