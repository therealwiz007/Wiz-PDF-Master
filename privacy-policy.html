Wiz PDF Master — Privacy Policy

Wiz Labs · Effective 16 November 2026

Your documents stay on your phone · No account

Summary

Wiz PDF Master scans documents, creates PDFs, reads PDFs and recognises text on your phone. Your scans, PDFs, signatures and recognised text are never uploaded by the app. There is no account and no sign-up. Wiz Labs does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

From version 1.5.0 the app has the internet permission. It is used only by Google components inside the app (described below) and to check Google Play for a newer version. The app works fully without a connection.

Camera

The camera is used only while you are scanning, to find page edges and take the photo you ask for. Images are processed on the device and saved inside the app's private storage.

Photos and files

When you import photos or open a PDF, you choose the file using Android's own picker. The app only gets access to the files you pick. PDFs you open from other apps are read in place and are not copied or uploaded.

Text recognition (OCR)

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit's on-device model, which is bundled inside the app. Recognition runs locally; no images or text are sent to Google or anyone else.

Google components

Google ML Kit and Google Play services may send Google limited technical information, such as the device model, Android version, app version and performance or crash diagnostics, to keep these features working. Your page images and their text are not sent. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

The optional Smart scanner, the extra text-recognition languages (Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) and QR / barcode reading are on-device components of Google Play services. Google Play services downloads these components to your phone itself, and your pages are processed on the phone. A link read from a QR code is opened only when you tap it, in your browser.

App updates

When you open the app, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if there is one, offers to update. This check is handled by Google Play.

Ads

This version shows no ads. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Passwords

If you unlock a password-protected PDF, the password is used only in memory to open that file and is never saved.

Storage and deletion

Your scans are stored in the app's private folder on your phone. Deleting a document in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Backups of app data are turned off.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Save to device, the PDF goes only where you send it. What happens after that is governed by the app or service you choose.

Children

Wiz Labs does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact Wiz Labs through the developer contact on the Google Play listing.
